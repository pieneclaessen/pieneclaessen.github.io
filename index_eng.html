A bit about me

Before data and AI, I was a military pilot. It taught me to stay calm under pressure, to plan carefully, and to never trust a system I hadn't checked myself — and that mindset has carried straight into how I work today.

Over the past six-plus years I've built data and AI solutions in places where the stakes were real: predictive maintenance models that kept operations running, AI assistants that put scattered knowledge back into people's hands, and data setups that made information something people could actually trust and act on.

Most data or AI projects don't fail because of the technology. They fail because nobody took the time to understand the problem first, or the tool was handed over without making sure it fit into daily work. That's the gap I try to close — I get to know your business, build something genuinely useful, and stay close enough afterward to make sure it sticks.
